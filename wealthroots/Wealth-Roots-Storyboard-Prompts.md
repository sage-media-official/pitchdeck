# Wealth Roots × Mukesh Gupta — storyboard prompt pack

15 prompts, one per reel. Each returns **one 3×3 grid = 9 storyboard frames** in reading order (1 top-left → 9 bottom-right).

**How to run each prompt**
1. Attach the watch photo named in the prompt (from https://pitchdeck-phi-weld.vercel.app/wealthroots/ — e.g. crop_green.jpg). For any glow frame, also attach crop_glow.jpg.
2. Paste the prompt. Generate.
3. If a frame changes the watch (round case, wrong colours, no screws, extra numerals) — regenerate that grid.
4. Send the 15 grids back; we split them into the deck.

**Rules that apply to every frame**
- The watch is ALWAYS worn on the man's wrist. Never lying on a floor, table, thali, cloth or any surface.
- Keep the watch exactly as the attached photo: square case with eight exposed bezel screws, layered case, WR monogram at 12, textured dial, colour-blocked rubber strap, crown on the right. Never round it, never add numerals, never change colours.
- The man: Indian, mid-30s, athletic build, short neat beard — a stand-in for Mukesh Gupta. No celebrity likeness.
- Locations: a Jaipur heritage haveli (jaali corridors, courtyard, rooftop over the old city, study, jharokha, blacked-out room) or free public places in Jaipur.
- Photoreal, cinematic lighting, real lens behaviour, shallow depth of field. No text, captions, logos or watermarks in the frames (only the watch's own WR monogram).
- Glow frames: near-total darkness, the ENTIRE case, bezel, hands and indices glowing bright green (match crop_glow.jpg), the glow the brightest thing in frame.
